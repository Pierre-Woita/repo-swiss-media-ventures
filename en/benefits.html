What we offer

Freedom with clear responsibility

Handing over operational tasks should relieve your day-to-day work and make managing your business easier. Swiss Media Ventures AG takes over the agreed operations and brings the parties involved together. You keep the strategic decisions in your hands.

  • Time for your priorities
  • Less coordination effort
  • Room for development
  • Your decision-making authority
  • Overview of operations
  • Relief through automation
  • Trust in the collaboration

More time for your priorities

The ongoing tasks of your platform do not have to come together with you every day. We take over the agreed operations. This gives you time for the entrepreneurial topics that require your personal attention.

Less effort between the parties involved

Managing several specialist companies and service providers takes time. Swiss Media Ventures pools the coordination and coordinates tasks across business units. You get central management of the agreed operations.

Capacity for the next step

New offerings, additional users and further markets change the requirements for your business. We take these developments into account in processes and resources. This way, operations can be prepared for your next steps.

You keep entrepreneurial decision-making authority

  • You decide: strategy, goals and the framework for approvals.
  • We take care of: operational planning, coordination and implementation within this framework.

Being able to follow operations

Responsibility is easier to hand over when authority and information channels are clear. Together we agree on goals, budgets and decisions that require your approval. Coordinated reporting shows the status of your business and makes the need for action visible.

Less manual work in everyday business

Recurring tasks tie up capacity. AI and automated workflows help evaluate information faster and reduce routine work. Our specialists manage the deployment and assess the results.

  • Evaluate business data faster
  • Handle recurring service requests more efficiently
  • Automate routine processes

Professional experience for your business model

Swiss Media Ventures combines technical, commercial and operational expertise for platforms and web shops with digital products and services. In personal dialogue we clarify which requirements are decisive for your business and how the specialists involved work together.
