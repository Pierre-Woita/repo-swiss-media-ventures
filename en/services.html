What we can do

The expertise for your operations

A digital business needs expertise in many different areas. Swiss Media Ventures AG pools this expertise and coordinates how it is deployed. Together we define which tasks we take on on your behalf and how the collaboration with your existing structures is organized.

  • Strategy & Corporate Management
  • Technology & Platform Operations
  • Product & Service Management
  • Marketing & Growth
  • Sales & Partnerships
  • Customer Service & Community
  • Server & Hosting
  • AI Engineering
  • People & Resources
  • Legal, Data Protection & Compliance
  • Processes & Quality Management

Strategy & Corporate Management

Your business goals set the standard for planning and management. We translate them into operational priorities, align budgets and coordinate the departments involved. AI-supported analyses condense business data and prepare relevant developments for decision-making.

  • Business planning
  • Management of goals and key figures
  • Coordination of teams and service providers
  • Management reports and decision-making foundations
  • Further development of the business model

Technology & Platform Operations

Maintenance, new features and ongoing operations must be aligned with one another. We coordinate these tasks and the technical service providers involved. Monitoring and automated checks help detect anomalies and assess the need for action.

  • Technical platform operations and monitoring
  • Maintenance and planning of software releases
  • Integration of interfaces and systems
  • Access management and data backup
  • Coordination of technical service providers

Product & Service Management

How your offering is used provides important clues for its development. We look after digital products and services in ongoing operation, evaluate demand and feedback, and derive concrete improvements from them.

  • Offer and product management
  • Design of pricing plans and subscriptions
  • Planning and coordination of new features
  • Quality assurance of digital services
  • Improving the customer experience from first contact to use

Marketing & Growth

We align communication and campaigns with winning and retaining your customers. Content, target groups and measures are coordinated with one another. AI supports content creation, target group analysis and campaign optimization.

  • Brand communication and content marketing
  • Search engine optimization
  • Management of paid campaigns
  • Email marketing and customer relationship management
  • Optimization of sign-up and conversion rates

Sales & Partnerships

From the first inquiry to the ongoing business relationship, sales needs reliable support. We organize the processes, coordinate offers and look after partners on your behalf. Intelligent assistance systems make research, prioritization and follow-up easier.

  • Handling and qualification of inquiries
  • Offer management and sales coordination
  • Support for business customers
  • Building and maintaining partnerships
  • Maintaining customer data and sales opportunities in the CRM

Customer Service & Community

Customers need clear answers and support that fits their concern. We guide onboarding, support ongoing use and moderate the community. AI-powered tools help with recurring questions; complex concerns are handled personally.

  • Introduction and support of new customers
  • Handling of support requests
  • Support for subscriptions and customer accounts
  • Complaint management
  • Community support and moderation

Server & Hosting

We provide the server and hosting environments of your platform and look after their operation. This includes maintenance, security and data backup as well as adjusting resources. Automated monitoring helps detect disruptions early.

  • Provisioning and configuration of servers and hosting environments
  • Monitoring, maintenance and updating of systems
  • Data backup, recovery and measures for failover protection
  • Securing servers, access and data connections
  • Adjusting resources as demand grows

AI Engineering

We develop and operate AI-based applications for your digital business. From selecting suitable models and connecting them to your systems to ongoing operation, our specialists make sure AI functions work reliably, transparently and in line with data protection requirements.

  • Analysis of suitable fields of application for AI in your business model
  • Development and integration of AI-based functions, assistants and automations
  • Connection to existing platforms, data sources and processes
  • Testing, monitoring and quality assurance of results

People & Resources

The requirements of your business determine which skills and capacities are needed. We plan the deployment of teams and external specialists, coordinate recruiting and organize onboarding and knowledge transfer.

  • Staff and capacity planning
  • Coordination of recruiting and onboarding
  • Support for external specialists
  • Organization of training
  • Building an internal knowledge base

Legal, Data Protection & Compliance

Legal and regulatory requirements have to be taken into account in day-to-day operations. We organize the relevant processes, keep deadlines in view and coordinate with legal, tax and data protection experts.

  • Contract administration and deadline monitoring
  • Coordination of data protection processes
  • Maintenance of policies and documentation
  • Organization of compliance reviews
  • Coordination with external advisors

Processes & Quality Management

How well workflows run is decided at the transitions between teams and business units. We define responsibilities, document processes and review their quality. Recurring steps are automated where this meaningfully relieves operations.

  • Documentation and improvement of workflows
  • Automation of recurring tasks
  • Definition of responsibilities and handovers
  • Quality controls and error analyses
  • Continuous improvement of operations
