Privacy Policy

1. Privacy at a glance

This Privacy Policy informs you about which personal data we process when you visit this website. Personal data is any data that can be used to identify you personally.

The controller is the legal person that alone or jointly with others determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data. Swiss Media Ventures AG is a company limited by shares (AG) under Swiss law.

3. Hosting and server log files

When you visit this website, your browser automatically transmits data to the server on which the website is operated. This data is stored temporarily in so-called server log files:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • page or file requested and amount of data transferred
  • status code of the request (e.g. “successful”)
  • browser type and version, operating system and, where applicable, the previously visited page (referrer)

This processing is necessary to deliver the website, to ensure its stability and security and to defend against attacks. Log files are deleted automatically, unless they are needed longer to investigate a security incident.

4. Fonts

This website uses the typeface “Fira Sans”. The font files are delivered from our own server. No connection to servers of font providers such as Google is established, and no data is transmitted to third parties in the process.

5. Cookies, analytics and marketing tools

This website currently only uses technically necessary cookies and no comparable technologies, and does not use analytics, tracking or marketing tools.

6. Contact and dialog

If you contact us by email, we process the data you provide (e.g. name, company, contact details, content of your inquiry) in order to respond to your request. We delete the data as soon as your request has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Through the multi-step question-and-answer dialog on our contact page, we process your answers to assess whether our services match your needs and to inform you of the result.

7. Recipients and transfers to third countries

We pass on your personal data only where this is necessary to perform our tasks, where we are legally obliged to do so or where you have given your consent. Recipients are in particular technical service providers (hosting, IT operations, email) that act only on our instructions as processors.

Our company is based in Switzerland, where we process personal data, as well as at the server location of our hosting provider. The European Commission has recognized Switzerland as providing an adequate level of data protection, so data from the EU or the EEA can be transferred to Switzerland without further safeguards. If we engage service providers in countries without adequate data protection, we ensure appropriate safeguards, in particular Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Data security

For security reasons, this website uses an encrypted connection (TLS/HTTPS). You can recognize an encrypted connection by “https://” in your browser’s address bar. We also take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorized access.

9. Your rights

Subject to the applicable data protection laws, you may in particular have the following rights with regard to your personal data:

  • to obtain information about whether and which personal data we process about you, and to receive a copy of this data
  • to have inaccurate data rectified and incomplete data completed
  • to have your data erased
  • to have the processing restricted
  • to receive your data, and to have it transferred, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format
  • to withdraw consent you have given, with effect for the future; the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal remains unaffected

The conditions, scope and possible exceptions of these rights are governed by the applicable statutory provisions.

To exercise your rights, you can contact us using the contact details above. Where necessary to match your request securely, we may request additional information to confirm your identity.

Right to object

Subject to the applicable data protection laws, you may object to certain processing of your personal data.

Where we process your data on the basis of legitimate interests and the applicable law provides a corresponding right to object, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop the processing concerned, unless there are legally permissible grounds for continuing it.

Where we process your personal data for direct marketing, you may object to this use at any time. We will then no longer use your data for this purpose.

Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe that the processing of your personal data violates applicable data protection laws, you may, in accordance with those laws, lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

Which authority is competent depends on the applicable law and the circumstances of the individual case. Depending on the jurisdiction, this may in particular be an authority at your place of residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. In Switzerland, the competent authority is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

You can also contact us directly with data protection questions or complaints. Contacting us beforehand is not a prerequisite for lodging a complaint with a competent authority.

10. Obligation to provide data

Visiting the website does not require you to actively provide personal data. The technically necessary transmission (see Section 3) is required to use the website. No automated decision-making, including profiling, takes place.

11. Up-to-dateness and changes to this policy

We update this Privacy Policy when the website, our processing or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
