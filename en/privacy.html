Privacy Policy

1. Data protection at a glance

The protection of your personal data is important to us. This Privacy Policy informs you which personal data we process when you visit this website and when you contact us, for what purpose and on what basis this takes place, and which rights you have. Personal data (“personal data” under Swiss law) is any information relating to an identified or identifiable person.

The most important points in brief:

  • This website uses no analytics, tracking or marketing tools and does not embed any third-party content.
  • When you access the website, technically necessary connection data is processed in server log files.
  • If you write to us via the contact form or by email, we use your details to answer your enquiry.
  • The images on this website are partly generated with the help of artificial intelligence. We provide information on this in section 9.
  • No automated decision-making and no profiling take place.

3. Applicable law and legal bases

We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the associated Ordinance (DPO). To the extent that the General Data Protection Regulation of the European Union (GDPR) applies to our processing, in particular because we direct offers specifically at persons in the EU or EEA, we also observe its requirements. In Switzerland, Art. 45c of the Telecommunications Act (TCA) applies to cookies and similar technologies.

Where the GDPR applies, we base our processing on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract and pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), if you make an enquiry about our services or initiate a cooperation;
  • legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), for example in the secure and stable operation of the website, in preventing misuse and in answering enquiries efficiently;
  • legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), for example retention obligations;
  • consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), where we expressly ask you for it in individual cases.

Under Swiss law, processing is based on the principles of Art. 6 FADP (lawfulness, good faith, proportionality, purpose limitation, transparency). Consent is required only where the law prescribes it; processing may also be based on overriding private interests.

Ticking the box “I have read the Privacy Policy” in the contact form confirms that you have taken note of this information.

4. Hosting and server log files

When you access the website, your browser automatically transmits data to the server, which is temporarily stored in so-called server log files:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of access
  • Page or file accessed and amount of data transferred
  • Status code of the request (for example “successful”)
  • Browser type and version, operating system and, where applicable, the previously visited page (referrer)

This processing is necessary to deliver the website, to ensure its stability and security and to defend against attacks. The log files are deleted automatically unless they are required for further investigation of a security incident.

5. Fonts

This website uses the typeface “Open Sans”. The font files are delivered from our own server. No connection is made to the servers of font providers such as Google, and no data is transmitted to third parties in the process.

6. Cookies, analytics and marketing tools

This website does not set any cookies and uses neither local storage nor comparable technologies for recognition. We do not use analytics, tracking or marketing tools and do not embed any external content (for example videos, maps or social networks). A consent banner is therefore not required.

Should we use such tools in the future, we will amend this policy and, where required, obtain your consent beforehand.

7. Contacting us

Contact form

If you use the contact form, we process the data you enter: subject of the enquiry, first and last name, company, where applicable position and phone number, email address and the content of your message. Required fields are marked; without these details we cannot process your enquiry.

The data is transmitted via an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards the enquiry to us by email. To protect against misuse (spam), the frequency of enquiries per device is limited; for this purpose, an irreversible check value (hash) is formed from the IP address and stored on the server only briefly.

Email and telephone

If you contact us by email or telephone, we likewise process the data you provide (for example name, company, contact details, content of the enquiry) to answer your enquiry. Emails may be read by third parties in transit; for confidential content we recommend a secure channel.

Purpose, legal basis and retention period

We use your details to answer your enquiry and to prepare a possible cooperation. We delete your data once the enquiry has been dealt with conclusively and no statutory retention obligations or legitimate interests (for example documentation of a business relationship) prevent this.

Multi-step dialog on the contact page

Through the question-and-answer dialog on our contact page, we process your details to assess whether our services match your needs and to inform you of the result. The statements above apply to purpose and retention period.

8. Recipients and disclosure abroad

We pass on your personal data only where this is necessary to perform our tasks, where we are legally obliged to do so or where you have consented. Recipients are in particular technical service providers (hosting, IT operations, email), who act only on our instructions as processors.

We are based in Switzerland and in principle process personal data in Switzerland and at the server location named above. If we disclose personal data to a country without an adequate level of data protection, this takes place only with suitable safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, or on the basis of a statutory exception. The European Commission has certified that Switzerland provides an adequate level of data protection; transfers of data from the EU to Switzerland are therefore possible without additional safeguards.

9. Images and AI-generated content

For the design of this website we use images that were generated or edited with the help of artificial intelligence programs (generative AI). These include in particular photographic motifs with people, landscapes and work situations as well as illustrations.

  • Fictitious depictions: People, situations and places in these images are entirely fictitious and do not depict real employees, customers or business partners. Any resemblance to living persons is coincidental and unintended. Where images look like photographs, they are synthetic depictions.
  • No processing of your data: No personal data of visitors to this website was used to generate these images. The website itself does not use any AI functions that evaluate your input or your usage behavior (for example chatbots or automated profiling).
  • Transparency: We point out the use of generative AI so as not to mislead you about the origin of the images. This also corresponds to the transparency principle of Art. 50 of the EU Regulation on artificial intelligence (AI Act), which provides for certain labelling obligations for AI-generated content, and to the principles of good faith and transparency under Art. 6 FADP.
  • Personality rights: If you believe that an image on this website depicts a real person recognizably or infringes your rights, please write to us. We will review your notice promptly and remove or replace the image where necessary.

Where our service descriptions refer to “AI-supported” analyses or automation, this concerns the services we provide to customers as part of a cooperation, not the processing of data when you merely visit this website.

10. Data security

For security reasons, this website uses an encrypted connection (TLS/HTTPS). You can recognize an encrypted connection by “https://” in the address bar of your browser. We also take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorized access.

11. Your rights

In accordance with the applicable data protection law, you have in particular the following rights:

  • Access to whether and which personal data we process about you, and to receive a copy
  • Rectification of inaccurate data and completion of incomplete data
  • Erasure of your data
  • Restriction of processing
  • Release or transfer of your data in a common, machine-readable format (data release or data portability)
  • Objection to certain processing, in particular to processing for direct marketing purposes
  • Withdrawal of consent given, with effect for the future; the lawfulness of processing carried out until withdrawal remains unaffected

The conditions, scope and possible exceptions of these rights follow the applicable statutory provisions. To exercise your rights, you can contact us using the contact details given in section 2. Where necessary to assign your request reliably, we may ask for additional information to confirm your identity.

Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe that the processing of your personal data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. In Switzerland, this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch). If you reside in the EU or EEA, you can also contact the supervisory authority of your place of residence or work or of the place of the alleged infringement. Contacting us beforehand is not a prerequisite. However, we would be glad if you gave us the opportunity to clarify your concern.

12. Obligation to provide data and automated decisions

Visiting the website does not require you to actively provide personal data. The technically necessary transmission of connection data (see section 4) is required to use the website. To process an enquiry via the contact form, the details marked as required fields are necessary. No automated individual decision-making within the meaning of Art. 21 FADP or Art. 22 GDPR and no profiling takes place.

13. Currency and amendment of this policy

We amend this Privacy Policy when the website, our processing or the legal situation changes. The version published here at any given time applies.
