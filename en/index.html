What we do

Implementing your goal step by step

In four steps we develop an understanding and take over the operational complexity and the ongoing operation of your digital business, coordinating technology, processes, marketing, development and expertise in one hand. Four steps give the collaboration structure: Analyze, Integrate, Operate, Optimize.

  2. We integrate

  3. We operate

    Fully committed to your business

  4. We optimize

    Overcoming obstacles. Opening up new paths.

What we can do

End-to-end for your digital business.

Running all areas of your business requires a wide range of tasks and skills, from development and technical operations to marketing and sales, customer service, HR and commercial management. We bring these areas together and coordinate them both personally and in an automated way. We agree priorities and responsibilities with you personally.

  • Strategy & Corporate Management

    We translate your goals into operational priorities and coordinate the business functions.

  • Technology & Platform Operations

    We look after the technical foundation of your business and coordinate operations, maintenance and further development.

  • Product & Service Management

    We support your digital products and services in day-to-day operation and develop the offering further based on usage and demand.

  • Marketing & Growth

    We make your offering visible and manage measures for customer acquisition and retention.

  • Sales & Partnerships

    We organize your sales processes and look after business partners on your behalf.

  • Customer Service & Community

    We support your customers from their first question through ongoing use.

  • Server & Hosting

    We host your platforms on secure, high-performance servers and ensure reliable operation.

  • AI Engineering

    We develop and operate AI-based applications and integrate them into your systems and processes.

  • People & Resources

    We coordinate the people and capacity your business needs day to day.

  • Legal, Data Protection & Compliance

    We organize the implementation of relevant requirements and coordinate collaboration with legal, tax and data protection experts.

  • Processes & Quality Management

    We connect the business functions through clear processes and unambiguous responsibilities.
