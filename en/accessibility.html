Accessibility

1. Our goal

Swiss Media Ventures AG wants its website to be accessible and usable for as many people as possible, including people with visual, hearing, motor or cognitive impairments and people who use assistive technologies. We orient ourselves by the guidelines for accessible web content WCAG 2.1, conformance level AA (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines of the W3C).

This statement is voluntary. For our offering to business customers, there is currently no legal obligation to publish an accessibility statement. We publish it for transparency and to enable feedback.

2. Scope

This statement applies to the website www.swiss-media-ventures.ch in its German and English versions. Not covered are digital platforms and web shops that we operate or support on behalf of our customers. The respective requirements and agreements with the customer apply to their accessibility.

3. Status of conformance

In our assessment, the website is partially compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA. The technical foundations are met (see section 5). We have not yet completed a full test under real conditions of use, in particular with keyboard and screen reader. We therefore do not currently claim full conformance.

4. How we tested

In [November 2026] we examined the website internally using an automated technical check, at various screen widths (320 to 1024 pixels). Among other things, we checked page language and title, heading structure, alternative texts, form labels, landmarks, text contrast on solid-colored areas, display on narrow screens (reflow), minimum sizes of controls and duplicate element identifiers. No independent external audit has taken place so far.

5. What has already been implemented

  • Every page has a language declaration, a unique page title and exactly one main heading (H1); the headings follow a logical order.
  • A “Skip to content” link makes it possible to bypass the navigation. Header, navigation, main content and footer are marked up as regions.
  • All content images have alternative text. Purely decorative images are hidden from assistive technologies.
  • Form fields have visible labels, required fields are marked, and error messages are announced as alerts.
  • Text contrast on solid-colored areas meets the required values. We use the red brand color on black only for large text.
  • The focus (keyboard indicator) is visible. Pages can be enlarged; zooming is not disabled.
  • Content remains readable without horizontal scrolling even at narrow widths (320 pixels); long words are hyphenated.
  • Controls such as the menu, sliders and anchor navigation are marked up as buttons and links and given a name. The click areas of the footer links and slider dots meet the recommended minimum size.
  • Animations are restrained; with the system setting “reduce motion” they are largely not executed.

6. Known limitations and outstanding tests

The following points have not yet been conclusively tested or may contain limitations:

  • White text on image areas: The contrast of text on tiles with a photo and a colored gradient cannot be measured automatically and has not yet been assessed manually.
  • Keyboard operation: Full operation of all elements (menu, sliders, anchor navigation, dialogs, form) using only the keyboard, as well as the focus order, have not yet been conclusively tested.
  • Screen readers: Output with common screen readers has not yet been systematically tested.
  • Text size and text spacing: Behavior at 200% text size and with increased line and word spacing has not yet been conclusively tested.
  • Sliders: Content that is reachable only by swiping or clicking sideways additionally offers buttons or dots; usability with assistive technologies remains to be confirmed.
  • English version: It has been tested to a lesser extent than the German version.

We remedy identified shortcomings step by step and update this statement after every test.

7. Images and AI-generated content

The images on this website are partly generated with the help of artificial intelligence. They serve illustration purposes and contain no information that is not also in the text. They are described with short alternative texts.

8. Feedback and contact

Do you have difficulties using this website, or do you need content in another form? Please write to us. It helps if you tell us the page concerned, a description of the problem and, if applicable, the assistive technology you use (for example a screen reader or magnification software). We usually reply within [5] working days and look for a solution together with you.

9. Note on the legal situation

This statement is not an assurance of conformity with any particular law or standard. It describes the status of our own testing. Accessibility obligations may arise for individual offerings from Swiss disability equality law or, for offerings to consumers in the EU, from the European Accessibility Act. They depend on the respective offering.
