Compliance

Our approach

Swiss Media Ventures AG operates and optimizes digital platforms on behalf of its customers. That trust requires us to act responsibly, lawfully and transparently. For us, compliance is not an end in itself but part of how we work and the basis of long-term partnerships.

The principles below apply to our management and all employees. We expect our business partners and subcontractors to follow them as well.

1. Compliance with laws

We comply with the laws and regulations that apply to us in every country in which we operate, including Swiss law and, where applicable, the laws of the European Union and other jurisdictions. Where the legal position is unclear or differs between countries, we take the more cautious course and seek expert advice.

2. No bribery or corruption

We do not tolerate bribery, corruption or improper advantages of any kind, whether involving public officials or in private business dealings. This includes compliance with the Swiss Criminal Code (Art. 322ter et seq., including bribery in the private sector under Art. 322octies and 322novies), the Federal Act against Unfair Competition and comparable foreign laws such as the UK Bribery Act 2010. Gifts, hospitality and other benefits may be given or accepted only if they are customary, modest, transparent and not intended to influence a decision. Facilitation payments are prohibited.

3. Fair competition

We are committed to fair competition. Agreements with competitors on prices, terms, markets or customers, and other violations of antitrust and competition law (e.g. Sherman Act, Art. 101 and 102 TFEU, German Act against Restraints of Competition), are prohibited. We advertise truthfully and make no misleading statements about our services.

4. Anti-money-laundering, sanctions and export controls

We enter into business relationships only with partners whose identity and integrity we have checked with appropriate effort, and we help prevent money laundering and terrorist financing. We comply with national and international sanctions and export control laws, including the Swiss Embargo Act and the ordinances based on it, the Goods Control Act and, where applicable, EU and UN sanctions, and do not provide services to sanctioned persons, organizations or countries where this is prohibited.

5. Data protection and information security

We process personal data lawfully, for specified purposes and in line with data minimization, in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR. We protect confidential information of customers and partners through appropriate technical and organizational measures. We report security incidents internally without delay and handle them as the law requires, including notifications to authorities and affected persons. See our Privacy Policy for details on this website.

6. Conflicts of interest and integrity

We base business decisions solely on objective criteria. Personal interests must not conflict with the interests of the company or our customers, and potential conflicts must be disclosed. We handle the assets and trade secrets of the company and our customers with care.

7. Respect, equal opportunity and human rights

We promote respectful cooperation and do not tolerate discrimination, harassment or retaliation, in particular on grounds of origin, race, sex, disability, religion or belief, age or sexual orientation or identity (e.g. Art. 8 of the Federal Constitution, the Gender Equality Act and Art. 328 of the Code of Obligations). We respect internationally recognized human rights and reject forced and child labor.

8. Responsibility toward business partners

We select subcontractors and service providers carefully and require them to follow the principles set out here. If we receive indications of violations, we review them and take appropriate action up to termination of the cooperation.

9. Voucher & Payment Compliance

Swiss Media Ventures AG issues its own digital vouchers, which can be redeemed exclusively on the websites we operate to pay for our own products and services.

Vouchers are non-transferable unless the applicable voucher terms provide otherwise, and cannot be redeemed for cash or used to make payments to third parties.

Vouchers are issued, administered and redeemed in compliance with applicable laws and with appropriate security measures to protect against misuse and fraud.

10. Reporting concerns

We encourage employees, customers and third parties to report suspected violations of laws or these principles. Reports are treated confidentially, reviewed carefully and, where legally possible, handled anonymously on request. Anyone who reports in good faith is protected from retaliation, in line with applicable law.

Contact reporting office

11. Responsibility and further development

Management is responsible for implementing these principles. We review our compliance measures regularly and develop them further.
