Compliance

Our self-image

Swiss Media Ventures AG operates and develops digital business models on behalf of its customers. This trust requires that we act responsibly, lawfully and transparently. For us, compliance is therefore not an end in itself, but part of how we work and the foundation of long-term partnerships.

The following principles apply to management and all employees. We also expect them to be observed by the specialist companies, service providers and business partners with whom we work. They are a short summary of our basic stance and do not replace legal advice or contractual agreements.

1. Compliance with law and regulations

We comply with the laws and regulations applicable to us, primarily Swiss law and, where applicable, also the law of the European Union and of other countries in which we or our customers operate. Where the legal situation is unclear or differs between countries, we take the more cautious path in case of doubt and seek expert advice.

2. No bribery and corruption

We do not tolerate any form of bribery, acceptance of bribes or granting of undue advantages, neither towards public officials nor in private business (among others Art. 322ter et seq. of the Swiss Criminal Code, including private bribery under Art. 322octies and 322novies CC, and comparable foreign provisions). Gifts, invitations and other benefits may be accepted or granted only if they are socially customary and appropriate and cannot influence business decisions. Benefits of significant value must be disclosed internally.

3. Fair competition and consumer protection

We are committed to fair competition. Agreements with competitors on prices, conditions, markets or customers, as well as other violations of antitrust and competition law (among others the Cartel Act, the Federal Act against Unfair Competition and, where applicable, Art. 101 and 102 TFEU), are prohibited. We advertise truthfully, make no misleading statements about our services and, in offers to end customers, ensure transparent prices, clear contract terms and compliance with applicable consumer protection rules.

4. Anti-money laundering, sanctions and export control

We enter into business relationships only with partners whose identity and integrity we have checked with reasonable effort, and we help to prevent money laundering and terrorist financing. We observe national and international sanctions regimes as well as export and technology control rules (among others the Embargo Act and ordinances based on it, the Goods Control Act and, where applicable, sanctions of the EU and the UN). We do not provide services to sanctioned persons, organizations or states.

5. Data protection and information security

We process personal data lawfully, for specified purposes and according to the principle of data minimization (among others the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where applicable, the GDPR). We protect confidential information of customers and partners by appropriate technical and organizational measures, such as access restrictions, encryption and data backup. We report security incidents internally without delay and handle them in accordance with legal requirements, including any reporting obligations towards authorities and data subjects. Details on the processing of personal data on this website can be found in our Privacy Policy.

6. Responsible use of artificial intelligence

We use AI tools to automate processes, analyze information and create content. The following principles apply:

  • Human responsibility: Results of AI systems are assessed by specialists. Significant decisions are made by people, not by algorithms.
  • Data protection and confidentiality: We enter personal data and confidential information of customers and partners into AI services only if this is legally permissible, contractually secured and necessary for the purpose.
  • Transparency: We label AI-generated content where this is legally required or necessary to inform those concerned. The images on our website are partly generated with the help of generative AI; the people and situations depicted are fictitious.
  • Third-party rights: We respect copyright, personality and trademark rights and review the terms of use of the services we use.
  • Regulation: We follow developments in AI regulation, in particular the EU Regulation on artificial intelligence and the rules planned in Switzerland, and adapt our practice accordingly.

7. Intellectual property and trade secrets

We respect the rights of third parties in texts, images, software, trademarks and other protected works and use them only within the scope of the required rights and licenses. We protect our own rights as well as the trade secrets of the company, its customers and partners with appropriate measures.

8. Conflicts of interest and integrity

We make business decisions exclusively on the basis of objective criteria. Personal interests must not conflict with the interests of the company or our customers. Possible conflicts of interest must be disclosed. We handle the assets and operating resources of the company and its customers with care.

9. Respect, equal opportunity and human rights

We foster respectful cooperation and do not tolerate discrimination, harassment or disadvantage, in particular on grounds of origin, gender, disability, religion or belief, age or sexual identity (among others Art. 8 of the Federal Constitution, the Gender Equality Act, Art. 328 CO). We respect internationally recognized human rights and reject forced labor and child labor.

10. Content, protection of minors and our customers’ platforms

We support digital platforms with offerings from entertainment, lifestyle and leisure. We help our customers comply with the requirements applicable to their offering, for example on the protection of minors, unlawful or discriminatory content, and information and labelling obligations. We review reports of unlawful content and take the necessary steps.

11. Responsibility towards business partners

We select specialist companies and service providers carefully and oblige them to comply with the principles set out here. In the event of indications of violations, we investigate them and take appropriate measures up to and including termination of the cooperation.

12. Reporting violations

We encourage employees, customers and third parties to report indications of possible violations of laws or of these principles. Reports are treated confidentially, carefully examined and, where legally possible, handled anonymously on request. Anyone who acts in good faith need not fear disadvantage, within the scope of applicable law.

Contact reporting office

13. Responsibility and further development

Management is responsible for implementing these principles. We review our compliance measures regularly, train employees where needed and continue to develop them.
