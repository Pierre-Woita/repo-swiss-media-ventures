Who we are

Experience meets responsibility

Swiss Media Ventures AG brings together specialized companies for the operation of digital business models. For owners of digital platforms, we take on the agreed operational tasks and coordinate the collaboration of everyone involved.

Swiss specialist for digital business models

Swiss Media Ventures AG is based in Switzerland and specializes in operating digital business models. We pool the many years of experience of our specialists and manage projects across the individual business units. In doing so, we combine the expertise of our network with clear responsibilities and automated processes that relieve ongoing operations. Your goals set the shared direction.

Internationally organized and centrally coordinated

Our network brings together specialists from technology, commercial management, marketing and operations. Their different perspectives complement each other in daily collaboration. Swiss Media Ventures coordinates everyone involved, aligns tasks and creates clear communication channels. This turns specialized knowledge into joint execution.

Modern technologies are the foundation

We combine technical infrastructure with the know-how for developing, operating and marketing digital offerings. This includes managed systems, coordinated interfaces and clear standards for implementation. By connecting systems and automating recurring tasks, we reduce manual work steps. Our specialists align these technical prerequisites with the requirements of product and marketing – so that your offering can be operated and developed further in a targeted way.

A collaboration begins with a conversation

Tell us about your business and your expectations of an operational partner. Together we examine whether our way of working and our expertise fit your requirements.
